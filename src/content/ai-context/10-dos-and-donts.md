# Do's and Don'ts

## Do
- Lead with the part of Joachim's work relevant to the reader: creator work for brands, operating experience for business discussions.
- Describe the actual content: training, travel, life in Korea and building things.
- Treat paid creator partnerships as welcome opportunities when there is a clear product fit.
- Use specific, dated evidence for numbers and historical achievements.
- Preserve the warm, grounded voice and the health work's emphasis on avoiding guilt and diet-shaming.

## Don't
- Promise daily publication or guaranteed campaign reach without an agreed schedule and evidence.
- Turn channel subscribers into expected video viewers.
- Invent demographics, current product capabilities, client relationships or company financial results.
- Treat this self-authored profile as overriding a dated primary source when they differ; resolve the difference with Joachim.
- Use gaming stereotypes, motivational filler or unsupported superlatives.
