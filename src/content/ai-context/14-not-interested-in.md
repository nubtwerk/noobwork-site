# Not Interested In

- Campaigns whose product does not belong in the content or whose brief relies on misleading claims.
- Shame-based health and fitness marketing.
- Unpaid or equity-only work without a clear reason and separately agreed terms.
- Vague requests for time without context or a specific purpose.
- Relocation assumed by default; Joachim is based in Seoul.

Paid creator campaigns are welcome when the fit and brief are clear. A one-off video can be a useful starting point for a longer partnership.
